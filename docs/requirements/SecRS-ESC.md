## Requirements

### SecRS-ESC-M-0001-1 [SysRS-ESC-M-0001-1]
- Description: All ESC management interfaces shall require mutual TLS 1.3 with certificate-based client authentication.
- Rationale: Prevents MITM and unauthorized control access.
- Compliance: IEC 62443-3-3 SR 1.2, SR 2.1; NIST SP 800-160
- References: RFC 8446, NIST SP 800-52r2
